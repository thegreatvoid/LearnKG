### S1: 7.3 Similarity by AA, SSS, SAS

7.3 Similarity by AA, SSS, SAS
The angle angle similarity postulate states that if two angles of one triangle are congruent to two angles of another, then the triangles are similar. The side side side similarity theorem states that if corresponding sides are proportional then triangles are similar. The side angle side similarity theorem states that if two sides are proportional and the included angles are congruent then triangles are similar.

### S2: 12.5 Composition of Transformations

12.5 Composition of Transformations
A glide reflection is the composition of a reflection and a translation parallel to the line of reflection. Isometries preserve angle measure, betweenness, collinearity, and distance. Any rigid transformation can be expressed as the composition of reflections.

### S3: 1.2 Segments and Distance

1.2 Segments and Distance
A line segment is named by its two endpoints. The distance between two points is the absolute value of the difference of the coordinates. A ruler postulate states that the points on a line can be matched with real numbers. Congruent segments are segments that have the same length. The midpoint of a segment is the point that divides it into two congruent segments. A segment bisector is any figure that intersects a segment at its midpoint. The segment addition postulate states that if B is between A and C, then AB plus BC equals AC.

### S4: 5.2 Perpendicular Bisectors and Angle Bisectors

5.2 Perpendicular Bisectors and Angle Bisectors
The perpendicular bisector theorem states that any point on the perpendicular bisector of a segment is equidistant from the endpoints. The angle bisector theorem states that any point on the angle bisector is equidistant from the sides of the angle. The circumcenter is the point of concurrency of the perpendicular bisectors and is equidistant from each vertex. The incenter is the point of concurrency of the angle bisectors and is equidistant from each side.

### S5: 8.3 Special Right Triangles

8.3 Special Right Triangles
A 45 45 90 triangle has legs that are congruent and a hypotenuse that is the leg times the square root of 2. A 30 60 90 triangle has a short leg, a long leg that is the short leg times the square root of 3, and a hypotenuse that is twice the short leg.

### S6: 4.2 Congruent Figures

4.2 Congruent Figures
Congruent polygons are polygons where all corresponding parts are congruent. The corresponding parts of congruent triangles are congruent is abbreviated CPCTC. Two triangles are congruent if their corresponding sides and angles are congruent.

### S7: 3.1 Lines and Angles

3.1 Lines and Angles
Parallel lines are lines in the same plane that never intersect. Perpendicular lines intersect at a right angle. A transversal is a line that intersects two or more coplanar lines. When a transversal crosses two lines, eight angles are formed. Corresponding angles are in the same position at each intersection. Alternate interior angles are between the lines on opposite sides of the transversal. Alternate exterior angles are outside the lines on opposite sides of the transversal. Co-interior angles are between the lines on the same side of the transversal and are supplementary.

### S8: 11.5 Spheres

11.5 Spheres
A sphere is the set of all points in space equidistant from a center. The surface area of a sphere is 4 pi radius squared. The volume of a sphere is four thirds pi radius cubed.
