Executive Summary & Core Research Questions

A rigorous forensic reality check on educational knowledge graph extraction.

The Central Scientific Finding

Can local, open-weight language models reliably discern directional prerequisite dependencies from textbook prose? We conducted a strictly controlled benchmark against the external human-annotated AL-CPL Geometry benchmark (1,681 pairs, 524 positives) using an identical common covered set (868 pairs).

Empirical Finding 1: Standalone Local LLMs Operate at Pure Chance
Without access to document position, an instruction-tuned 7B model (Qwen-2.5-7B) achieves an ROC-AUC of 0.503 [95% CI: 0.49, 0.51] and a directional accuracy of 50.0% (4 out of 8 positives decided). Textbook sentences define properties symmetrically, offering insufficient directional cues for neural models.
Empirical Finding 2: Reading Order Carries an Overwhelming Inductive Prior
A zero-parameter baseline sorting concepts by first appearance in the textbook achieves F1 = 0.736, ROC-AUC = 0.868 [95% CI: 0.81, 0.91], and puts the prerequisite first for 251 out of 260 true positives (96.5% directional accuracy). Any study that omits reading order risks attributing chronological correlation to neural reasoning.
Empirical Finding 3: Multi-Criteria Saaty AHP Weighting Is Functionally Redundant
Evaluating edge-ranking formulas against AL-CPL queries shows that Saaty's Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP, W = 0.52S + 0.24C + 0.09P + 0.15E, CR=0.022) achieves NDCG@10 = 0.709, which is identical to sentence co-occurrence alone (C = 0.723, p=0.68). Definition lexical overlap (S, weighted at 52%) scores 0.589, performing no better than random tie-breaks (0.582).

The Core Research Questions

  • RQ1
    Local LLM Efficacy: How accurately do local 7B LLMs extract directed prerequisites compared with deterministic rules and structural baselines?
  • RQ2
    Bottleneck Cascade: At what pipeline stage (concept discovery, candidate generation, or classification) are dependencies actually lost?
  • RQ3
    Neuro-Symbolic Value: Does the hybrid pipeline succeed due to neural semantics or almost entirely from reading-order gating?
  • RQ4
    Weighting Validity: Does Saaty's multi-criteria AHP formula provide genuine ranking value beyond simple co-occurrence?

Deep Dive 1

Concept Discovery Tradeoff

rule_v3 uses 130 curated terms, missing short acronyms (< 3 chars like "HL" theorem). kgx_v2 extracts 204 concepts via noun-chunks, but dropped foundational terms like "area" and "similarity" due to a single-word frequency filter.

Deep Dive 2

Candidate Pairing Bottleneck

Intra-sentence co-occurrence causes a 43.3% loss in ground-truth prerequisite recall. Cross-chapter dependencies are impossible to capture if models are only given single sentences to evaluate.

Deep Dive 3

What Does the LLM Add?

The LLM provides an undirected relatedness filter (boosting edge precision from 0.595 to 0.845 by pruning spurious early concepts). However, its marginal contribution to directional ROC-AUC over reading order alone is just +0.020.

Stage 3 • Parallel Multi-Signal Scoring Engine (Percentile Rank Fusion)
Signal A • Structural
Reading-Order Forward Gate
Forward-only: $p(u) < p(v)$, scaled by the character position gap. Zero score if $p(u) \ge p(v)$.
Dominant Directional Signal
Signal B • Distributional
Sentence Co-occurrence
Percentile rank of raw sentence co-mention frequency across the textbook corpus.
Primary Ranking Driver
Signal C • Literature Baseline
RefD Co-Citation Rank
Reference Distance (Liang et al. 2015) computed over sentence mention sets.
Statistical Baseline
Signal D • Neural
LLM Relatedness Rank
Sum of symmetric relation scores from `kgx_v2`: $\text{score}(u,v) + \text{score}(v,u)$.
Undirected Filter Only
Combined Score = Forward Gate × Mean(Percentile Ranks of Signals B, C, D)

Top-k Predecessors Filter
kg_extract/hybrid.py
Retains only top-$k=3$ predecessors per concept that are also related ($\text{cooc} > 0$ or LLM related), preventing early concepts from connecting to everything.
Top-3 Degree Bounding
Stage 5 • Consistency
DAG Cycle Elimination
kg_extract/consistency.py
Enforces strict DAG acyclicity on Prerequisite edges via topological feedback arc set sorting.
Guaranteed Acyclic

kg_extract/hybrid.py
